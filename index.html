New · Live Chat Desk

When a customer wants a person, a person is one click away

A small button at the bottom-right of every panel page. It lights up when someone asks for you — on your website or on WhatsApp.

  • Accept and chat — see who's waiting, what they said and the AI's summary so far. Click Accept and you're talking; the first teammate to accept gets it, no double replies.
  • Same number, same window — on WhatsApp your reply goes from your business number; website visitors see "connecting you to a person…" and then you.
  • Hand it back or close it — return the chat to the AI, assign it to a colleague, or resolve it. A summary lands on the customer's record either way.
  • Nobody free? — the owner gets a nudge after two minutes; website visitors are asked for a number and a call-back task is created for you.

New · Invoicing & payments

From "deal won" to paid in full

Raise a proper tax invoice the moment a deal closes — without leaving the CRM or opening another app.

  • Real GST invoices — HSN/SAC codes, per-item units, buyer and seller GSTIN, CGST + SGST or IGST split, round-off to the rupee and the amount in words.
  • Drafted by AI — type "website 15k + one year hosting 5k, 18% GST" and the line items appear. Edit anything, then save.
  • Sent where customers are — a clean PDF on WhatsApp or email in one click, with your bank details printed on it.
  • Payments tracked — record part or full payments; see invoiced, received, pending and overdue at a glance.
  • Tied to the deal — invoices attach to a contact or to a record that reached its final stage, so nothing gets billed twice.
